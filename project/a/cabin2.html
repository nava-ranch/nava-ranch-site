Building type: cabin_base.

Build sequence

1. Setting out and excavation: Laser setting out of grid lines (north line >= 1 m from the C15 crest), test pit, footing holes below 0.9 m.

2. Foundations: Bored piles on the north line, pad footings with rebar, Sonotube piers, embedded anchors, all tops at +/-3 mm.

3. Longitudinal beams: Treated 160x200 beams on post bases, splices over supports, leveled and anchored to every pier.

4. Frames: assembly and tilt-up: Frames built flat on a jig (rafters, tie, plywood gussets, collars), tilted up with the excavator, braced, brackets and screws at each foot.

5. Sarking and underlayment: OSB3 15 sarking nailed as a diaphragm on both slopes, vapour-open underlayment the same day, temporary X straps.

6. Roofing: Counter battens (40 mm vent gap), battens, black trapezoidal sheet with anti-condensation felt, flashings, ridge vent.

7. Gables and partitions: East gable and partitions framed 50x150, west gable with mullions and triangular glazing frames, plywood, WRB, furring.

8. Windows and doors: Fixed triangular glazing, entrance door and 2 windows, sealing tapes.

9. Floor: Rodent mesh and wind barrier under the ties, 200 mm glass wool, vapour control, plywood 18 T&G glued and screwed.

10. Interior: Roof and wall insulation, vapour control taped, service battens and wiring, pine T&G ceiling, interior log siding, loft, stair, finish floor, plumbing, furniture.

11. Exterior cladding: Exterior log siding on vented furring (never directly on the sheathing).

12. Decks: Posts on bored piles, beams, joists, decking. Independent of the cabin structure.
